# Product

> Inferred from the founder's brief (no interview run). Assumptions are marked.

- **Product:** Saphi, a mobile-first web app for planning and caring for gardens and home harvests.
- **Users:** Landscapers and people starting out in gardening who have little time. Not commercial farms or professional agronomists.
- **Core job:** Draw a garden to scale, place plants with their real size, and see which plants crowd each other, shade each other, need different soil pH, or are invasive.
- **Tone:** Calm, minimal, trustworthy. No data exploitation, no social feed.
- **Visual direction (pinned by founder):** Glassmorphism, minimalist, mostly white, dark green and brown accents, day and night palettes by local time.
- **Navigation:** Bottom bar with Home, Garden, Harvest, Profile.
- **Assumption:** First market is Argentina (Southern Hemisphere, sun from the north).
